# AuctionHouse update — 08 Nov, 2026

Latest follow-up: [reference flowchart, account separation, Token Money, interval and reminder fixes](RELEASE-2026-11-08-FOLLOWUP.md). This supersedes the earlier direct-bidding summary cards and arbitrary negotiation increments. No migration beyond 0007 is added.

The implementation and rebuilt deployment package are local. No production migration, deployment, credential change or real customer email has been performed by this task.

## Customer website

- Home uses the linked lime ballot flow: LDA application → successful applicant → Sell now; unsuccessful applicant → Marketplace. The old hero posters are removed. The requested Pine Axis/Pine Executive line and opportunity underline are present. Project cards include the LDA application link.
- Notifications have confirmation-based deletion, property title/ID and bidding ID metadata where applicable, and no email-delivery status label. A notification unrelated to a property says so; an unassigned bidding ID is not fabricated. Bid text distinguishes the customer's own bid from another bidder's bid and identifies the actor's bidding code. Popup close buttons sit outside navigation links, do not navigate, and popups dismiss after six seconds. A short sound plays after a browser-permitted user interaction; browsers may suppress autoplay before interaction.
- Negotiation state, offers and reminders are restricted to their two parties. Negotiations no longer pause public bidding or increment/broadcast a public property version. Multiple buyers can negotiate privately with the seller. Private accepted amounts are not returned to unrelated public viewers. Closing a property remains public; private negotiations are not disclosed to other bidders.
- Facts and Terms opens in a dialog. Auction terms do not show the negotiation interval or response-time paragraph. Participation still requires current terms acceptance and explicit bid confirmation.
- Supporting-document uploads accept PDF only, on both client and server. Uploading does not leave a required empty file input blocking submission. Uploaded filenames and document links remain visible. Salient features use checkboxes.
- Saved searches, alerts and the saved-search account tab are removed. The old API returns 410 and the migration clears existing saved searches. Marketplace omits the price-unit explanatory sentence and single-page pagination. Multi-page pagination remains below results.
- The bidding cost-estimate module and text WhatsApp share action are removed. A share icon appears below the heart, beside a contrasting land-use pill. It uses the device's share sheet where available, with a copy-link/WhatsApp fallback otherwise. Third-party app availability is controlled by the browser/device.
- Area-conversion paragraphs are removed from cards/details. ID, title and address spacing is tighter. Opening and closing dates include Pakistan time in AM/PM format, labelled PST as requested. Seconds appear only in countdowns. The current Pakistan time appears near the closing time.
- Bid history appears in the bidding panel immediately after entry/bid controls. A customer with previous bids gets the next three amounts directly on property details. My Biddings supports confirmed bids without opening the property.
- My Properties replaces My Listings. My Biddings and My Properties are separate header destinations between Notifications and My Account, with lime and sky-blue borders and unseen-activity stars. They are available inside the menu on narrower screens. My Account retains interests, deals, balance and settings.
- Custom-property titles use `Plot No. <number>, <block>, <scheme>` and their address field displays land use. Optional blank blocks are omitted.
- Orange action backgrounds use white text. Existing typefaces are retained.

## Refundable bid tokens and fees

- Project tokens are set in AH Projects, shown in the seller form, and reserved atomically with project submission. Insufficient available balance prevents submission without creating an unfunded project property. Tokens are shown before bidding and reserved before participation. Insufficient funds create a notice directing the customer to hello@auctionhouse.pk, 0339-2828466 or the office.
- Custom properties submit for review before their token is known. The administrator specifies the token during review, including when sending observations or rejection. Successful review with insufficient seller funds leaves the property pending with `awaiting_token`; it is not published. Once funded, the seller can explicitly reserve the token and publish the reviewed property.
- Token balances use integer paisa and distinguish total, reserved and available money. Reopening can reserve a released token again. Reservations are not disguised as cash payments. Existing published property/token commitments are not silently repriced.
- New accepted property deals store the final total plot price and buyer/seller fees, each at 0.5%. Per-unit amounts are converted to total plot price before calculating fees with integer arithmetic. Closed Deals displays both fees. Fees and participation describes this policy. Previously issued legacy agreements and their ledgers retain their accepted terms; the legacy Fees & Settlement admin module is hidden.

## Negotiation and reminders

- Repeated +/- adjustments and direct offer entry are supported. Both parties' amounts must be at least 40% and below 100% of the starting price. A buyer cannot reduce their own last offer; a seller cannot increase their own last offer.
- Either party can reopen an expired response window while the property remains open, using a strictly better own offer: a higher buyer offer or a lower seller offer. A closed property cannot reopen.
- Each party can send at most two private reminders for a negotiation turn. A current highest auction bidder can remind the seller after one hour, once for that highest bid. Authorization, timing and limits are enforced server-side.
- A no-bid property's negotiation-request action becomes available after its holding period without requiring a new bid event. Seller-enabled direct entry remains available independently.

## Administration

- Each customer has a unique six-character hexadecimal public user ID; internal relational IDs are preserved. Existing IDs are backfilled, and new customer IDs are allocated with uniqueness checks. Last customer activity is recorded with a short write-throttle.
- Users Management provides the requested table, field sorting, default activity-descending order, 25 rows by default, up to 1,000 rows per page, search, activity filters, all/filtered Excel exports and a row detail screen. Exports contain profile, balance, property, bidding-ID, closed-deal and cash-history details. Exports are authorized and audited.
- User details show exact property names/IDs, bidding IDs, closed deals and cash records. Finance-authorized staff can edit a cash record through an append-only correction. Original receipts remain intact and available balance cannot fall below reservations. Corrections require a unique reference and reason.
- Permanent customer deletion removes the personal profile and access, and purges supporting documents. Active commitments, outstanding balance or unpaid legacy entitlements must be cleared first. Anonymous financial identifiers/records remain for ledger integrity; deletion does not erase or forgive financial obligations.
- Project visibility is an Enable/Show checkbox. Hiding a project removes it from active project choices; existing transaction history is retained. Added seller fields can be deleted. Page Settings supports Marketplace project-tab positions and featured properties. Featured results sort before ordinary results.
- Pending property review supports editable property entries and a proposed draft. It highlights old/new changed values for the seller. A seller must accept or reject; rejection requires a non-empty reason. Approval cannot bypass an outstanding seller draft response. Published auction facts/pricing cannot be edited silently through this pending-review route.
- Admin modules show per-administrator unseen-activity stars. General Facts & Terms is a separate module; custom sellers receive editable defaults.
- New customer and admin passwords accept any eight or more characters, with no letter/digit composition rule. New password hashes prehash before bcrypt so long Unicode passwords are not silently truncated; existing bcrypt passwords remain valid. Descriptive text has no arbitrary minimum length beyond non-empty required fields. Identity formats, numeric ranges, structural identifiers and upload/payload safety limits remain validated.
